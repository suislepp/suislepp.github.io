I'm more of a fast learner and a decent judge of tradeoffs than a deep specialist, so I'm at my best where the problem is new and messy rather than where it needs someone who knows the internals inside out.

Beyond the job description

Most jobs I've had, I end up building some small tool nobody asked for. A wallpaper generator at a bank helpdesk, a PowerShell CLI toolbox, a Jira sentiment analyzer, and this year podrunner at the healthcare platform. If it makes my or my coworkers' lives easier, I'll build it.

Noticing when something's off

I'm pretty good at catching logic gaps and inconsistencies in big messy contexts, configs, docs, audit evidence, whatever. Fairly often it's something that already passed a review, which makes me either useful or a bit annoying depending on the day.

Writing things down
